# DPIA draft (Art. 35) — Identity documents

## Processing

Collecting passport scans, passport numbers, parents' names, addresses, signatures from international students after payment.

## Necessity

Support identity consistency for partner introductions and fraud prevention. **Default recipients: NIVO admins only** (GAP-IDENTITY-PURPOSE).

## Risks

High sensitivity ID documents; non-EU students; potential unauthorised access or over-retention.

## Mitigations

Encryption, AV scanning, masked display, audited reveal, retention 30/90, deletion/export, DPIA review with lawyer, no partner sharing without consent + DPA.

## Status

Draft — finalize when GAP-IDENTITY-PURPOSE and lawyer texts close.
